# Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## AI & ML Internship

### 1. Objective
To understand the dataset using statistical methods and visualizations, identify patterns and trends, explore relationships between features, and detect potential anomalies.

### 2. Dataset Information
- **Dataset Name:** Shopify Sales Dataset
- **Dataset File:** `shopify_sales_dataset_ml_eda.csv`
- **Total Records:** 60,000
- **Original Columns:** 17
- **Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Platform:** Google Colab

### 3. Tasks Performed

The following activities were completed as part of Exploratory Data Analysis:

1. **Summary Statistics**
   - Calculated mean, median and standard deviation.
   - Examined minimum, maximum and quartile values.
   - Analyzed numerical and categorical features.

2. **Histograms and Boxplots**
   - Created histograms to understand numerical data distributions.
   - Used boxplots to examine data spread and potential outliers.

3. **Feature Relationships**
   - Created pairplots to visualize relationships between numerical features.
   - Generated a correlation matrix and heatmap.

4. **Patterns, Trends and Anomalies**
   - Analyzed monthly revenue trends.
   - Compared revenue across product categories.
   - Identified potential outliers using the IQR method.

5. **Feature-Level Inferences**
   - Interpreted product price, quantity, discount, revenue, profit and customer ratings.
   - Used visualizations to understand important data patterns.

- Average discount percentage is approximately 18.15%.
- Average revenue is approximately 994.10.
- Average profit is approximately 980.58.
- Revenue and profit distributions show potential high-value outliers.
